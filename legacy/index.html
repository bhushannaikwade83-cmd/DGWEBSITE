FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a project take?

A typical website takes 3 to 6 weeks, an app or AI project 2 to 4 months. We give you a firm timeline after the discovery call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. After a short call we send a clear quote with fixed milestones, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere and meet over video calls.

Will I own the code and designs?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the source code and design files are yours.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and improvement plans so your product keeps getting better.

Have something to build?

Tell us about it. We'll reply with a plan.
